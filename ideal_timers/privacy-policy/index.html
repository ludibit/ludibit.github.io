Ludibit Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This policy explains how Ideal Timers, developed by Ludibit, handles your information. Contact: iamludibit@gmail.com.

Quick summary

  • Ideal Timers stores your content locally. We do not run servers to store or sync it.
  • You choose whether to sync or export backups, which devices to sync with and where to save or share exported files. Device backups follow your operating-system backup settings.
  • AdMob processes advertising information. We do not send your timer content to AdMob.
  • On Android, Google Analytics measures installs and ad revenue so we can see which creator links and ads work. It follows your consent choices and never receives your timer content.
  • Android includes ads and has no in-app purchases. On Windows, Microsoft Store handles the paid app purchase.

Your local data

Ideal Timers requires no app account. It stores boards, timers, schedules, counters, history, notes, settings, keyboard shortcuts and selected wallpaper locally to provide its features. It also stores a random sync installation identifier and, on Windows, cached Microsoft Store license status. Names and notes can contain personal information you enter. Timer content may appear in notifications and background controls according to your device settings.

Storage and sync of this content do not use a developer-hosted cloud service. The app does not request your name, email, contacts, precise GPS location, camera or microphone to run timers.

Optional sync, exports and backups

Sync connects directly to paired devices on your local network and exchanges encrypted backups containing boards, timers, history, notes, settings and available shortcuts. Category settings control what is applied, but do not remove other categories from the transmitted backup. Device discovery and connection details, such as device names, identifiers, network addresses and service ports, are not encrypted by the sync protocol.

Pairing secrets, device details, timestamps and saved sync versions remain locally for reconnection and conflict resolution. Removing a paired device removes its local pairing record and saved versions, but not copies on that device. Use trusted devices and networks.

Exports are readable, unencrypted JSON files containing app database content, including the installation identifier. Mobile sharing creates a temporary copy. You choose where to save or share files; receiving services follow their own policies. Import replaces database content. Your operating system may separately back up or transfer app data under its settings and provider.

Android advertising and privacy choices

Google AdMob processes IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location; identifiers such as advertising and app-set IDs; interactions such as app launches, taps and video views; and diagnostics such as launch time, freezes and energy usage. Google describes advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as processing purposes. Processing depends on SDK behavior, device settings, consent and ad configuration, and may involve Google and advertising partners. Google’s consent service also handles information needed to manage privacy choices.

The app does not supply timers, notes, history, boards, settings, schedules, wallpaper, shortcuts, backups or other content created or saved through its features to AdMob. This is separate from SDK device, interaction and diagnostic information.

Before the app explicitly initializes the advertising SDK or requests ads, Google’s consent platform checks requirements, displays required forms and determines whether ads may be requested. When required, “Advertising privacy” in Settings lets you review your choices. Android also provides advertising-ID controls.

See Google’s Privacy Policy, partner-services explanation and AdMob data disclosure. This policy does not itself obtain consent.

Android analytics and promotion measurement

On Android, Ideal Timers uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how people find the app and whether our promotion works, for example which creator link or ad led to an install, and how much ad revenue the app earns. Google Analytics processes an app-instance identifier, the advertising ID, approximate location derived from a masked IP address, app launches and sessions, the Google Play campaign or link that led to the install, and ad revenue reported by AdMob.

The app does not send timers, notes, history, boards, settings, schedules, wallpaper, shortcuts, backups or other content created or saved through its features to Google Analytics, and it logs no custom events.

Analytics stays off until the app has checked your privacy choices. Where Google’s consent platform asks for consent, for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, analytics runs only if you allow Google to store information on your device and measure advertising performance; otherwise it stays off. When required, “Advertising privacy” in Settings lets you change your choices. Ad personalization from this data is always disabled, and we do not build remarketing audiences.

We link Google Analytics with Google Play, Google AdMob and Google Ads only to report installs, ad revenue and the results of our own campaigns. Google processes this data under its terms for these services, and we have turned off optional sharing of Analytics data for Google’s own purposes. Analytics event data is kept for 14 months and then deleted automatically. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

On Windows, the app contains no analytics. If you install or buy through a Microsoft Store promotional link, Microsoft may report to us how many installs and purchases that link produced.

Purchases

The Android app has no in-app purchases. Microsoft Store handles the paid Windows app purchase; the Windows app has no additional in-app purchase. Microsoft Store processes payment and provides app-license status used to recognize ownership, and Windows uses the Store’s license information and offline cache. We do not receive full payment-card details. See Microsoft’s policy.

Retention and deletion

Local content remains until deleted, replaced by import or removed with app storage. History defaults to 90 days and is pruned during startup and history operations. You can change retention or clear history; a non-positive retention value disables automatic pruning.

“Reset all data” resets the database. It does not remove every separate file, including saved sync devices/versions, wallpaper, shortcut preferences and exported backups. Remove saved devices and wallpaper through their controls. On Android, clearing app storage removes app-private data; uninstalling generally does too, but system backups may restore it. Desktop uninstalling may leave the data directory. Delete exports, backups and copies elsewhere separately.

On Android, you can ask us to delete the Google Analytics data linked to your installation: open Settings > Advanced > “Delete analytics data” and send the anonymous ID shown there to iamludibit@gmail.com. We request deletion of that ID’s data from Google Analytics within 30 days and confirm by email; Google may need additional time to complete removal from its systems. If no ID is shown, no analytics data has been collected on that device. Advertising data is processed by Google under its own policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Resetting does not cancel purchases or erase third-party records. Google, Microsoft and backup/sharing providers retain information under their own policies.

Support, diagnostics and security

Emails and attachments you send are used to respond and kept without a fixed automatic deletion period. Request deletion at iamludibit@gmail.com and we will delete the requested correspondence from our mailbox. Your copies and email-provider backups follow their own retention practices.

Apart from Google Analytics on Android, described above, there is no developer-operated analytics or automatic crash-upload service. Diagnostic builds may keep local logs; errors and state changes may appear in system/developer logs. Review logs before sharing. Local databases and exports are not encrypted by the app; device security and your chosen destinations affect protection.

Audience, website and updates

Ideal Timers is a general-purpose timer app, not specifically designed for or marketed to children.

The policy page uses GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical requests, including IP addresses, under its Privacy Statement. We add no analytics, ads, tracking scripts or cookies to the page.

We will update this policy and its effective date when data practices change. Privacy questions and requests: iamludibit@gmail.com.
